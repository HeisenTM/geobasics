Most Obvious Countries

Some countries give themselves away with one clue. Learn these first and you will win many rounds in seconds.

  1. Check the basicsDriving side, license plates and the Google car come first.
  2. Look at the roadsidePoles, bollards and road lines point to a country quickly.
  3. Confirm with languageUse the alphabet and special letters to finish the guess.

    No country matches that search. Try a different word or clear the filters.

    One clue is not always enough

    Neighbors can look alike, so open each country to see what to check next. Combine clues, like plate color plus driving side plus language. Car clues such as tape, snorkels and antennas can change in newer coverage, so never rely on them alone.
